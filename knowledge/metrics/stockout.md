# Projected stock-out
The first unmet demand in a batch-aware simulation of consumption, expiration, reservations and confirmed arrivals. Hourly estimates assume uniform consumption within each daily forecast interval.
No shortage in the next 28 days means no projected stock-out within the evaluated horizon, not unlimited coverage.
Supplier arrival dates are assumptions. Unconfirmed orders are excluded.
