# Suspected incident response
A report is operational evidence, not a confirmed diagnosis. Independent consumption anomalies can corroborate it. Zone circles are planning boundaries, not predictions of infection spread.
Only completed historical observations enter the model. Unknown future outbreak values are never covariates.
A reported requirement or an observed surge can raise the seven-day planning floor. Overlapping evidence is not added twice. A report with no quantity triggers a watch, not an arbitrary multiplier.
